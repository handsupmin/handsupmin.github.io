Support

Help for field proof capture.

FieldProof Camera creates local proof packets from camera, time, location, project notes, and export files. It has no account system and no app server, so most issues are solved on the device.

Common Fixes

Camera

Camera not enabled

Use Open device settings from the Capture screen, allow camera access for FieldProof Camera, then return to the app and tap Setup.

GPS

No GPS lock

Allow location while using the app, move outdoors if needed, then tap Refresh GPS before exporting.

Share

Cannot share a report

Create a proof packet first, then open Packets and share the ZIP bundle. If a client needs a specific format, share the stamped image, HTML report, CSV, or JSON individually.

Before Contacting Support

  • Confirm the project or job number field is filled before export.
  • Check that camera, location, and photo-library save permissions are allowed. The Capture screen can open device settings when camera or location permission is missing.
  • From Settings, copy or share support diagnostics if you want to include app version, platform, permission state, coarse proof counts, and settings flags.
  • User-initiated support diagnostics exclude photos, notes, project names, file names, paths, hashes, and coordinates.
  • Include your app version, device model, operating system version, and the action that failed.
  • Do not send client-sensitive proof photos unless support specifically asks for a redacted example.

Contact

Use the support email listed in the App Store or Google Play listing. If a storefront does not show an email yet, use the developer contact listed on that storefront.
