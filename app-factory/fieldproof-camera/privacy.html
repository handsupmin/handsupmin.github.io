Privacy Policy

FieldProof Camera is a local-first utility for creating field photo proof packets. It does not require an account, does not use runtime AI, and does not upload proof files to an app server.

The app may process camera images, GPS coordinates, timestamps, project labels, notes, stamped images, ZIP bundles, CSV files, JSON files, and HTML report files on your device so you can create and share proof records.

Data The App Handles

Camera

Camera and photos

Used to capture proof images and save stamped proof images when the user chooses that export behavior. The app does not browse the user's photo library.

GPS

Location

Used, with permission, to stamp proof records with coordinates and GPS accuracy.

Local

Local proof content

Project labels, notes, generated files, and proof history remain on device unless the user shares them.

Analytics

FieldProof Camera uses Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics for Firebase to understand installation, onboarding, activation, proof export, purchase funnel, retention, and error events. Analytics events do not include raw proof photos, full report contents, raw project notes, full hashes, local file paths, raw error messages, or exact street addresses.

You can turn off diagnostic analytics from Settings. When disabled, FieldProof stops Firebase/GA4 analytics collection on that device.

Settings also lets you copy or share support diagnostics when you choose to ask for help. These user-initiated support diagnostics include app version, platform, permission state, coarse proof counts, and settings flags. They exclude photos, notes, project names, file names, paths, hashes, and coordinates.

Firebase may process app instance identifiers, device information, coarse usage signals, and diagnostics according to Google's applicable terms and policies.

Sharing and Retention

  • Proof files are stored locally on the device.
  • Shared CSV and JSON proof records use safe file names and hashes instead of app-internal file paths.
  • The app uses the device share sheet when the user chooses to share a ZIP bundle, image, report, CSV, or JSON file.
  • Support diagnostics are copied or shared only by the user and do not upload proof content automatically.
  • Photo library access is limited to saving stamped images, not reading the user's existing library.
  • The app does not request microphone access, background location access, or system overlay access.
  • Deleting a proof packet removes its local history entry and associated app-owned files when available.
  • Settings includes a clear local data control for local proof history and app-owned proof files.
  • Uninstalling the app removes app-owned local data according to the operating system's behavior.

Contact

For privacy or support requests, use the support email listed on the App Store or Google Play listing, or visit the support page linked from this site.
