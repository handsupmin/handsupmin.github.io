Streak Rescue Privacy

Local habit recovery, minimal collection.

Local data

Your rescue action, completion state, and recent rescue history are stored locally on your device. Streak Rescue does not require an account and does not upload habit text to an app server. Android Auto Backup is disabled for this app so local habit data is not restored through Google Drive by default. If you copy a local backup from Settings, that backup includes your rescue text and history so you can store it yourself. The backup is created only when you tap the copy action. If you restore a backup from your clipboard, the app replaces local rescue content on this device and turns daily reminders off until you enable them again.

Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics to measure coarse events such as first open, saving a rescue plan, completing the core action, settings open, and day return signals. Analytics is used to improve the product and store listing. The Android advertising ID permission is blocked in the app manifest. You can turn diagnostic analytics off in the app Settings on this device.

Support diagnostics

If you copy or share support diagnostics from Settings, the text is generated locally and includes app version, platform, analytics/reminder status, and counts. It excludes habit text, history text, URLs, notification ids, raw errors, and device ids.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily rescue reminder, Streak Rescue schedules a local notification on your device. The reminder text is generic, does not include your habit text, and does not use a remote push server or collect a push token in version 1.

Runtime AI

Streak Rescue has no runtime AI feature. It does not send your habit text to an AI provider and does not operate as an AI wrapper.

Camera and location

Streak Rescue does not request camera access or location access. If a future version adds a permission, this policy and the store privacy labels must be updated before release.
