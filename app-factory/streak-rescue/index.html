Streak Rescue

Missed yesterday? Rescue today.

A private paid app for people who quit a habit after one missed day. Write one small rescue action, set a local reminder if needed, and mark the day recovered.

For People who spiral after breaking a habit streak.
Relief One action small enough to complete today.
Cost shape No account, no server, no runtime AI wrapper, no push token.

The app starts at the failure moment.

  1. Name the tiny rescue action.
  2. Save it on this device with an optional local 9:00 reminder.
  3. Mark it done and see the recovery in local history.

Built for low operating cost and low shame.

Streak Rescue keeps habit text local on the device. Optional diagnostic analytics can record coarse activation and retention events after purchase, without account data or runtime AI processing, and can be turned off in Settings. Reminders are scheduled locally on the device.
