Streak Rescue Support

Help for the day after a missed habit.

Using the app

Write one rescue action that is small enough to finish today. Save the rescue plan, then tap the completion button after you do it.

Data storage

Rescue text, completion state, and recent rescue history stay local to your device. Reinstalling the app or wiping device data can remove local records. If the app says local storage was repaired after a read issue, copy or share Support diagnostics before contacting support.

Daily reminder

The optional 9:00 reminder is a local notification. If it does not appear, check notification permission for Streak Rescue in device Settings. If permission is turned off outside the app, Streak Rescue turns the local reminder setting off the next time the app opens or resumes.

Support diagnostics

In Settings, tap Copy or Share under Support diagnostics before contacting support. The copied or shared text includes app version, platform, analytics/reminder state, and counts, but excludes habit text, history text, URLs, notification ids, raw errors, and device ids. If the app shows the Recovery screen after a display problem, try the in-app recovery action first; if the issue returns, open Support from that screen and include copied support diagnostics.

Contact

For support, refunds, privacy questions, or store review issues, contact the developer through the support address listed on the App Store or Google Play listing.
